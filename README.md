Basically, its steal an egg but educational

▶️ **Play it here:** https://afreakydapizza.github.io/animal-rescue-game/

## Places in the game
- 🏠 **Hub**: your park, with the Safety Zoo, Reproduce Machines, shops, stalls and the Magic Library
- 🌳 **Biome 1**: Deforestation Site
- 🌊 **Biome 2**: Polluted Ocean
- 🐦 **Biome 3**: Bird Poaching Site
- 🧊 **Biome 4**: Arctic Meltdown
- 📦 **Biome 5**: Smuggler's Compound
- ⛰️ **Biome 6**: Mining Mountain
- 👑 **Biome 7**: Enchanted Kingdom (with mushroom parkour to the Isotope Angel)
- 🏖️ **Secret Beach**
- 🧪 **Laboratory** and the **Boss Arena**
- 🎃 **Halloween World**: Ghost Town, Graveyard, Haunted House, Pumpkin Reaper Dragon
- 🔥 **Hell**: Witch Castle and the **Endless Witch Tower**
- 🤠 **Catch & Tame**
- 💪 **Ranger Gym**
