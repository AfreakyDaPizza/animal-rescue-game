# 🎤 Demo Day Cheat Sheet

Game link: **https://afreakydapizza.github.io/animal-rescue-game/**

## Before you start (at school)
1. Open the game link in **Chrome**. Press **Ctrl + F5** once so you have the newest version.
2. Plug in a **mouse** if you can. It is much easier than a touchpad.
3. Turn the sound on. There is music and lots of sound effects!
4. If the game feels slow, press **G** to change the graphics quality.

## A 3-minute demo route
| Time | What to do | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | The opening cutscene plays. Press a key to skip if you are short on time. | "I'm a ranger who rescues endangered animals." |
| 0:20 | Stand on the **Treadmill** (Speed) and sit at the **Student Table** (IQ) in the Training Zone. | "You train to get faster and smarter." |
| 0:40 | Walk into **Biome 1**, find the glowing animal, and press **E** to borrow it. Run back to the Hub! | "The boss chases you, so you need Speed." |
| 1:10 | Hit an obstacle: a quiz pops up. Answer it (try a **📚 Word Power** question!). | "Every question teaches a real fact or a school word." |
| 1:30 | Back in the Hub you see the **knowledge card**. Put the animal in a **Reproduce Machine**, collect the pet, then put it on your **Plot** to earn money. | "Real facts: every animal has its real IUCN status." |
| 2:00 | Press **I** for the **Index** with pictures of every animal. | "There are almost 60 animals to find." |
| 2:20 | Show something cool: the **Enchanted Kingdom**, the **Gym**, or the **Royal Skywhale** (see below). | "This is the newest update!" |
| 2:50 | Finish: "Animals need our help. Speak up and take action!" | |

## Show the new stuff early (Secret Ranger Control Panel)
The secret button is on the **Secret Beach** (it opens after you beat the Lab boss), behind the **round grey rock** near the forest edge. Get really close and press **E**.
- 🔮 **Future → 🚀 Unlock ALL future updates** opens the Fuse Machine, the Enchanted Kingdom and the Gym.
- 🔮 **Future → 🏃 Take me to the Gym** teleports you to the Gym. It skips the Lab boss if you have not beaten him.
- 🐾 **Pets** gives you any pet. Try a **GIANT x10, 🔥🌑, 🙃 upside-down Royal Skywhale**!
- 🎉 **Events** starts an event right now. Giant Pets and Rare Rush look great in a demo.

No time to reach the beach? The button behind the **golden rhino statue** (near the park gate) gives you enough Speed for every biome, plus lots of money. Your IQ becomes 0, though.

## If something goes wrong
- **Stuck, frozen or a weird screen?** Press **Ctrl + F5**. Your save is kept.
- **The mouse doesn't turn the camera?** Click on the game once, or press **Esc** and then click **Click to Play**.
- **You used the Control Panel and want your normal game back?** Open the panel, go to **💾 Safety** and press **Restore my real save**.
- **Lost?** Walk back towards the Hub, or borrow an animal and let the Boss catch you, which flings you back to the Hub. 😄
- **It's very slow?** Press **G** to lower the graphics, and close other tabs.
